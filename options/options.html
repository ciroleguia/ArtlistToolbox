Keywords

One term per line. Terms match whole words (case and accents ignored). Clear a list to disable it.

Penalized terms

Videos containing these lose score (unless the Artlist track mentions them too).

Preferred terms

Videos containing these get a small bonus (max +10%).

      Page & library

      Language Applies to popup, settings, rankings, menus and in-page overlays.
      Hide promo banner Removes the big promo hero on library pages before first paint.
      Compact rows Tighter track and SFX rows so more of the library fits on screen.
      Dim processed rows Subtly fade tracks you already processed.
      Keyboard shortcuts d = download hovered · c = copy info · 1–5 = rate · 0 = clear · t = tags/note · f = focus favorites · / = search · q = queue.

      Track rankings

      Enable track rankings Rate Music & SFX rows with 1–5, add tags/notes, and focus favorites. Works with the download flow off.

        Advanced

        Show CSV export button in popup Adds the "Export visible tracks (CSV)" button to the popup. Right-click export stays available either way.

        Modifier clicks & menus

        • Alt + click — always show the picker for that track.
        • Shift + click — only open the YouTube search for that track.
        • Ctrl/Cmd + click — copy the resolved YouTube link (no tab).
        • Right click on a track — copy its info from the context menu.
        • Right click on the page — export visible tracks as CSV or open the Rankings side panel.
